# Bitrot test checklist

Set up once: Chocolate Plus on **U**, USB cable, CubeSuite footswitches A to D momentary CC 20 to 23, expression on CC 11. In Ableton's MIDI preferences turn Track, Sync and Remote off for the footswitch. Load Bitrot on an audio track. The dot by FOOTSWITCHES should be green. On the Pedal page press **Calibrate** and rock the EX-P heel to toe once.

## Footswitches

- [ ] **A tap**: bypass toggles, red light goes out and back.
- [ ] **A hold** while on: silent while held, back on when you let go. From bypass: plays while held, off again on release. Stabs answer the moment you press.
- [ ] **B tap**: the sub card steps −1 OCT → −1 OCT · 5 → … → −3 OCT · M2 → back round. **B hold**: steps back one.
- [ ] **C tap** on a held note: oscillator and sub freeze; play other notes and the square follows over the drone. Tap again to release. Pebble goes still with a frost outline.
- [ ] **C hold**: freeze only while held. The frozen pitch is the note you were on when you pressed, even if you moved as you stamped.
- [ ] **D tap**: oscillator jumps an octave (OCT UP lights, Pebble stretches tall). Tap again to drop. **D hold**: up only while held.
- [ ] **A+B together**: next preset. Bypass doesn't change.
- [ ] **C+D tap**: nothing about the sound changes (with two Bitrots loaded, the footswitches move to the other one).
- [ ] **C+D held 1 second**: "KEPT" appears, no freeze, no octave. A new entry is in Documents / Flip Fella Keepers.

## Expression (EX-P)

- [ ] Heel = UNISON, toe = +3 OCT, the ladder steps through all eight harmonics as you rock.
- [ ] Rest the pedal halfway: the interval doesn't flicker between two steps.
- [ ] With Glide and a slow Rate, rocking smears the steps together.
- [ ] Click the expression bar, choose **Dry**: heel is pure synth, toe brings in your clean signal.

## Bass tracking

Input **BASS**, Square and Subharmonic up, Oscillator on +1 OCT.

- [ ] Open E, A, D, G: the oscillator lands on each note immediately with no swoop or octave jump. The tracking strip says LOCKED.
- [ ] Low B (five-string) and drop D: locks within about three cycles; no wobble while the note rings.
- [ ] Neck pickup soloed (strong second harmonic): no octave errors.
- [ ] Fast legato line on the E and A strings: each note lands; no lag building up.
- [ ] Sub on −3 OCT · M2 on open E with **Floor ON**: a pitched note (about 37 Hz), not a flutter. Floor OFF: the 4.6 Hz pulse of the original.
- [ ] Switching Input to Guitar turns Floor off; back to Bass turns it on.
- [ ] Notes stop dead when the string dies, with no hum or chatter in the gaps.

## The glitches

- [ ] Corrupt to WILD, play double stops: the loop hunts and stutters, Pebble glitches (colours split, torn rows, pixel eyes), tracking shows LOST.
- [ ] Wild, let a note fade: the oscillator dives and chirps before cutting out.
- [ ] Sub Root OSCILLATOR on +2 OCT: sum and difference clang; on the Deep page DIVIDED is cleaner.
- [ ] Vibrato at high Rate: laser.
